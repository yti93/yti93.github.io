Life Outside FinOps

I firmly believe in analog hobbies to complement a career that lives entirely in dashboards and spreadsheets. Here's what fills the rest of my time.

Golf

A pandemic-era hobby that's grown into a favorite way to spend a weekend. What started as something to do when everything else was closed has stuck around long after.

Surfing

I surf Redondo Beach ("RAT" — RAT Beach) most often, but my favorite break in Southern California is San Onofre.

Gardening

Trying my hand at growing my own vegetables and herbs — partly because I'm tired of grocery store produce spoiling before I use it, and partly because it's a good excuse to get outside and feel good about a little honest work.

Cooking

I love cooking new cuisines and trying new recipes with my family. I've been building out my outdoor kitchen and experimenting with different recipes and cooking methods along the way.

Reading

Mostly fiction — Terry Pratchett's Discworld is my favorite series, and I've been on a Brandon Sanderson kick lately. I also really enjoy essay writing, especially Jia Tolentino.
